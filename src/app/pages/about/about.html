THE BEGINNING

Born in Melbourne's North

Founded in 2021 by a group of passionate coffee roasters and hospitality veterans, The Northern Grid Cafe set out with a simple mission: to bring city-standard specialty coffee and premium artisan dining to the northern corridor of Melbourne North.

We believe that a cafe is more than just a place to get caffeine—it's a community anchor. That is why we designed our space to be warm, inclusive, and energetic, bridging the gap between quality culinary arts and neighborhood comfort.

OUR CORE VALUES

What Drives The Grid

Ethical Micro-Lot Sourcing

We work directly with green coffee importers who pay fair wages to farmers across Colombia, Ethiopia, and Brazil. Our rotating single-origins represent the unique terroir of their farms.

Seasonal & Local Produce

Our kitchen menu is built around seasonality. We source our milk from Victorian dairy farms, our bread from local sourdough bakers, and our herbs and produce from sustainable local farms.

Zero-Waste Initiatives

We compost all coffee grinds and offer them free to local Melbourne North gardeners. All of our take-away cups, straws, and packaging are 100% biodegradable and compostable.

OUR HOME

Find Us on Plenty Road

We are centrally located in Melbourne North, directly accessible by the Route 86 tram (Stop 68). Our cafe features free customer parking at the rear and a spacious pet-friendly outdoor courtyard.
